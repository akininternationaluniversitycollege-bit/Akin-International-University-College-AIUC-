About Akin International University College

Akin International University College is a new, globally connected institution dedicated to providing accessible, high-quality education to students in Liberia and beyond.

Our mission is to empower students with knowledge, skills, and global opportunities through free online courses, partnerships with top universities, and career support.

Our Vision

To become a leading African university recognized for innovation, accessibility, and global impact.
